## 3. Differences that change website copy

Rule of thumb for every item: when the Android fact differs, either write the platform-neutral sentence given here, or name the platform. Never carry the iOS wording over to Android. Items 1, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 16 changed since the first pass; every item was re-checked at `b6c576db`.

1. Pixel Art: no platform difference left (the first pass said Android had no lives; that was true at `4c80b609` only). At `b6c576db` Android has the iOS model: 3 lives on every difficulty, a wrong cell costs a life and becomes a locked cross, a right cell locks, a completed row or column crosses its empty cells, undo takes back crosses only, stars by errors (0, 1, 2 or more errors = 3, 2, 1 stars), Easy starts with five 5x5 levels then 8x8 (Medium 10x10, Hard 12x12, Elite 15x15), tutorial page 3 says a wrong cell costs a life, Premium infinite lives apply, and a second chance by ad or gems returns 2 of 3 hearts. Rows: "Grid sizes", "Undo", "Lives and locking", "Stars", "Tutorial", "Premium infinite lives scope", "Lives, ten games", "Stars by errors".
   - Wording true on both: "Pixel Art: read the clues on each row and column, paint the grid, and watch the picture turn to color when you finish. You have three lives: filling a wrong cell costs one. A hint corrects a mistake before it reveals anything."
   - The iOS fact-file wording can be used as it stands for Pixel Art. Any copy drafted from the first-pass file ("no lives on Android") must be redrafted.
   - Unproven on a device: the lives flow itself (recette T22 unplayed, `docs/port/pixelart.md:16`); the statements above rest on code, JVM tests and the Android port sheet.
   - For the Premium sentence use "infinite lives in the games that have lives" (Android: seven games, Pixel Art among them).
2. Evening reminder. iPhone and iPad: "a Live Activity on the lock screen counts down the time left to save your streak, from 8 pm to midnight". Android: "a notification counts down the time left to save your streak, from 8 pm to midnight" (it appears only if you have a streak, have not played yet and allowed notifications; on Android 13 and later the app asks permission). Android also sends streak warnings at 1 pm, 7 pm and 9:30 pm. Re-verified at `b6c576db`, code unchanged.
3. Screen reader. iPhone and iPad: VoiceOver (the file also claims Switch Control and Voice Control). Android: write nothing about TalkBack, screen readers or accessibility. The code has TalkBack semantics on most boards, but the victory "Continue" and tutorial "Next" buttons are still listed open as invisible to TalkBack and Switch Access (item B42, no re-measure recorded), Trace and Maze boards expose no per-cell action, Cross Math is not completable with Switch Access on either platform, and no TalkBack pass on a device is recorded for the 3.0.0 work.
